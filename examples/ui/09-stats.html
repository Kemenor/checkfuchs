One Stats screen, made of tiles. Every tile is a lens-card with a small uppercase title and a drag handle; the tune in the title bar opens Edit tiles, a switch list in display order — off tiles sit at the bottom, and new tiles can be appended later without touching the screen. Seven tiles to start: Completion, This week, Streaks, Insight, Habit grid, By window, Misses by weekday (the last three carry the same marks as before: emerald heatmap, per-window bars, amber-highlighted weekday columns). The palette law holds inside each tile: a miss is a dashed taupe outline, never red; skipped is a struck grey ring; only done gets colour, and every state also differs by shape. Stats is a View in the nav; the Stats tab switch in Settings (default on) only hides the tab — the tiles and their order are kept.
