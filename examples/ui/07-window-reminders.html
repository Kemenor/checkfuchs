Model. Window chips are a set. One preset = today's behaviour. Two or more = the union of bands; the task is one instance, done once in whichever band you catch it in (want it twice a day? make two tasks). Custom… is additive: every tap appends a from → to band row, so a day can hold as many bands as you like, presets included; × removes one band. Reminders are a list: the three presets stay one-tap chips and appear as read-only rows; Add a reminder appends an inline row with a day-offset stepper (On the day · 1 day before · 2 days before…) and a clock time — no popup. Each row is removable; un-ticking a preset chip removes its row. Engine side: bands are a list of Slices, presets are (anchor, offset), a custom row is an absolute time resolved per occurrence from its day offset.
