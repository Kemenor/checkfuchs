Why a pager first. The current sheet asks for a habit before the user knows what a lens or a view is, so the words on Home (“Daily habits”, the view tabs, “New lens here”) land cold. Three pages, one idea each, drawn from the app's own shapes — the task row with its ring, the dashed lens outline, the tabbed screen — so the illustration is the UI they're about to see. Copy stays under two lines; Skip is always available; the last page's button leads straight into the unchanged carrier-habit form. Indigo (the lens-focus colour) is the pager's accent so the intro reads as “about the model”, and the orange primary button stays for the one action that matters.
