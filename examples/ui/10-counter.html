  • Ring: one segment per check-in up to max (or min when there's no limit). Below min the filled segments are fox orange; once met they turn emerald; at max the ring becomes the ordinary Done check.
  • Pill: progress in tabular figures — 1 / 2 for a fixed goal, 1 / 2–3 for a range, 9 · NO LIMIT past the minimum. Orange below min, emerald once met.
  • Swipe: the Done action reads +1 on a counter; Skip shows only below min.
  • Removing a check-in re-resolves: Done at 3/3 → back to met while the week is open; a closed week that drops below 2 becomes Missed.
