The idea

A to-do app that doesn't die on a busy day.

repeat

Habits carry the app

One unavoidable daily habit gets you opening it every day — and your dated tasks and backlog ride along, never silently vanishing.

spa

"Not done" is data, not a verdict

A missed habit just fades — it never nags or bleeds red. The only real failure is a hard deadline you let pass.

center_focus_strong

Lenses & Views

A Lens picks and orders a slice of your tasks; a View arranges the Lenses you care about into a screen. A calm Home, a detailed Habits tracker — dials you set, not rigid categories.

event_repeat

One object, calendar-smart

A one-off becomes a series in two taps; "every other Saturday" is easy; editing this occurrence vs. the whole series works like a calendar — no surprises behind your back.

Fully on-device — no accounts, no analytics, no server, no ads. Dark + light, four languages, and a typeface picker with dyslexia- and low-vision-friendly fonts. Part of the Fuchsbau family.
